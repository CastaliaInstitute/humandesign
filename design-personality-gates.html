Design & Personality Gates

A personal way to read the two sides: Personality is “what I think of myself”; Design is “what other people might notice” in me. In Human Design terms, these are the conscious and unconscious/body activations. Each counterpart is the channel’s other end, and its status shows whether the channel is defined in your combined chart and which side(s) activate each end.
