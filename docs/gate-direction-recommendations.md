# Gate-by-gate direction recommendations

## How to read this recommendation

Human Design gives a gate a theme and places it at one end of one or more channels. It does not assign each gate one permanent source or sink role. A gate can therefore be the proposed receiver on one channel and the proposed source on another. The direction recommendations below are **per channel**, then restated for every gate so the gate inventory can be reviewed directly.

These are recommendations for the BodyGraph's particle-flow visualization, not a claim that Human Design describes literal one-way energy transport. “Evidence” distinguishes:

- **Stream clue:** a Jovian Archive teaching names a directional stream or column between centers. This supports the center-to-center orientation, but does not always prove every constituent channel is a one-way pipe.
- **Channel clue:** the reviewed Human Design material describes the specific channel directionally.
- **Interpretive:** the proposed orientation is a readable sequence inferred from the channel and gate themes. It needs confirmation against channel-specific teaching.
- **Cycle / leave undirected:** the themes imply feedback, reciprocity, or a start/finish cycle; a single arrow would overstate the evidence.

Biology does not resolve gaps in Human Design evidence. Neural pathways have circuit-specific afferent/efferent directions; endocrine signals travel to receptor-specific targets and use feedback; circulation is looped. Those facts support modeling different processes differently, not using a universal biological direction for BodyGraph channels. The particles remain an abstract visualization unless a specific biological process is explicitly being modeled.

## Recommendation summary

- Use the **stream-clue** arrows as the most defensible one-way candidates, while retaining the caveat that a stream direction is not automatically a physical particle pathway for every channel.
- Keep the five integration/relationship cases **10–20, 10–34, 57–10, 34–57, and 21–45** undirected for now; use a visible feedback loop only where the UI can distinguish it clearly from a one-way channel.
- Also leave **37–40** and **42–53** undirected: their reciprocity/cycle themes make a single arrow under-supported, and 42–53 contains mixed clues.
- Treat all other non-stream proposals as **tentative interpretive arrows**, not established Human Design mechanics. Before changing the SVG, verify each against channel-specific source material and keep its evidence label in the data/documentation.
